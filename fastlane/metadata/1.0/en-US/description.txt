Turn videos and Live Photos into animated cutouts, then arrange them into your own moving scenes.

Create a project on one canvas:
• Cut people out of videos and Live Photos on your device (iOS 17 or later)
• Layer multiple cutouts; adjust position, size, rotation, order, and timing
• Add stickers, text, backgrounds, frames, and visual effects
• Extract audio from a video, place it on the timeline, and adjust volume and fades
• Export as GIF, transparent HEVC video, or H.264 video
• Save projects on your device, then export to Photos or share

Cutout generation, composition, and export happen on your device. No account is required, and the photos and videos you choose are not uploaded.

GIFBloom is a creative tool for making and editing personal animated photo projects.
