# Generated Template Artwork

Generated visual template artwork for the LivingFrame project. Put future generated templates in this folder. Name each asset `<template-name>-<ratio>.png` using `16x9`, `1x1`, or `3x4` suffixes.

## Classic opening concepts

- `studio-medallion`: red velvet, gold film medallion, and title plaque inspired by the MGM cartoon opening composition. The original user-provided MGM Cartoon screenshot is the primary visual reference. Historical context: [MGM cartoon title cards](https://cartoonresearch.com/index.php/mgm-cartoons/) and the academic study [I'll Get You, Tom and Jerry (and That Little Dog, Too!)](https://ualresearchonline.arts.ac.uk/id/eprint/18699/3/I%27ll%20Get%20You%20Tom%20and%20Jerry%20And%20That%20Little%20Dog%2C%20Too.pdf).
- `cartoon-iris`: concentric color-ring title card inspired by the classic Merrie Melodies / Looney Tunes opening device. Historical references: [Looney Tunes and Merrie Melodies filmography (1929–1939)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Looney_Tunes_and_Merrie_Melodies_filmography_(1929%E2%80%931939)) and [Warner Bros. Pictures Animation](https://www.warnerbrospicturesanimation.com/).

## Wanted poster concept

- `wanted-bounty`: original Western bounty poster with a large rectangular transparent portrait window. The three aspect ratios adapt the poster composition while keeping the portrait window prominent.

## New frame concepts

- `photo-frame`: ornate antique walnut and gold photo frames with a rectangular transparent portrait opening and transparent space outside the frame.
- `newspaper-front`: aged newspaper front pages with a rectangular transparent portrait opening, letterpress headlines and editorial columns.
- `wizarding-academy`: original midnight-blue and gold celestial portrait frames with stars, moon phases and alchemical ornament. The design uses generic magical motifs rather than franchise logos or characters.

Each family is provided in `16x9`, `1x1`, and `3x4` ratios. Keep the portrait window rectangular and truly transparent, and adapt its placement and surrounding composition to the aspect ratio.
