# Halloween circular-mask templates

Four selected square PNG masks for the classic sticker-template category. Each image has a transparent circular center opening and transparent exterior. The 1254×1254 images are included in the app as 1:1 templates; no other aspect-ratio variants were generated. Measured center openings are approximately 48–53% of the canvas width, close to the existing cartoon-iris template.

- `ghost-parade.png` — friendly ghost, pumpkins, candy, lavender and cream.
- `black-cat-moonlight.png` — black cat, bats, crescent moon and pumpkin.
- `candy-web.png` — friendly spider, candy corn and a wrapped-candy medallion.
- `little-witch-cauldron.png` — witch hat, potion cauldron, moon and stars.

These are the source copies of the assets included in `Packages/LivingFrameCore/Sources/LivingFrameCore/Resources/GeneratedStickers/Templates/`. They are static images with no animation frames.

The visual direction uses current Halloween motifs and styles reported for 2026: friendly ghosts and the classic ghost/bat/pumpkin/black-cat mix, nostalgic pumpkin lanterns, whimsical storybook looks, and playful color palettes. References:
